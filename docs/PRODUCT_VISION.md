The authoritative, versioned principles are in Neon `atlas.current_principles` (`who-cares-product`); repository mirror: [WHOCARES_PRODUCT_NORTH_STAR.md](https://github.com/Strunden/whocares/blob/data/problem-atlas-foundation/WHOCARES_PRODUCT_NORTH_STAR.md). ## Product

Who Cares is an **evidence-backed problem discovery and market intelligence experience for venture builders**, founders and entrepreneurs-in-residence exploring ageing, care and related systems.

**Information → Understanding → Self-directed opportunity exploration.**

The product must make the complex landscape legible, not decide on behalf of the user what they should build.

## User, not subject

The **user** is a venture builder developing a mental model of an unfamiliar space and looking for a credible direction to investigate. The **subjects** include older adults, professional caregivers, informal/family caregivers, care organizations, clinicians, payers, communities, policymakers and innovators. Do not conflate them.

The venture builder's jobs:
1. **Orient:** rapidly comprehend the breadth of ageing and care, major problem spaces, whose lives/work they affect, and why they exist.
2. **Discover:** encounter important, unfamiliar, non-obvious problems without knowing the right search term.
3. **Understand:** follow lived experiences and workarounds alongside institutional incentives, workflows, money flows and causes.
4. **Inspect responses:** find interventions, products, services and their documented limits, without assuming market supply equals solution efficacy.
5. **Form their own thesis:** identify what they want to investigate next, what might be worth building, and which assumptions need field validation.

A successful session leaves the user with a **more accurate mental model and a useful next research question**, not a generated attractiveness score.

## Ethical north star: A Life Worth Living

The goal is to expand people's real ability to live and work in ways they value. This spans **all affected parties**, including fulfilling, sustainable careers for caregivers, manageable responsibilities for relatives, dignified autonomy for older adults, and economically sustainable organizations and innovators. It must not impose a single universal view of a good life.

This is a **criterion guiding research and responsible solution thinking**, not a mandatory ontology or a navigation menu, and never a numerical moral score. Show tensions honestly: safety versus autonomy, staff capacity versus personal attention, affordability versus provider sustainability, etc. Not every socially important problem offers a viable startup model, and not every high-margin intervention creates acceptable human outcomes.

## Product boundaries

- **Not** a conventional company directory with editorial category labels.
- **Not** an AI-powered ranking engine or venture investment committee.
- **Not** a linear awareness-raising publication that hides the bigger landscape.
- **Not** a diagnosis-driven medical encyclopedia.
- **Not** a fabricated-persona story generator or unsourced market-size visual.

## Non-negotiable discovery principles

- Problem landscape is researched independently of existing products.
- Bottom-up lives and workarounds and top-down institutions and economics are complementary, sometimes conflicting explanations.
- The same problem may affect many people and connect to several themes.
- Company records document **existing responses**, not taxonomic authority.
- Research candidates, documented findings, interpretations and unknowns remain distinct.
- The venture builder retains agency to weigh potential commercial, human and operational consequences.

## Product success checks (internal, not exposed ranking)

After a short unprompted visit, can a newcomer explain major landscape forces, identify who is affected, explain at least one surprising cross-connection, and choose a problem to explore? After a deeper visit, can they explain mechanisms, current workarounds/responses, evidence limitations, and a testable next question? Check false confidence, not just engagement.
