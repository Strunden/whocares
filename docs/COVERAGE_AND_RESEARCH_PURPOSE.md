## What a purpose supplies

A landscape map need not select a venture, but it needs a bounded question and intended use. The atlas user's question is: **Which bounded ageing-and-care problem should I investigate further, with whom, and on what evidence?** Each investigation needs a more specific population, concept and context. “All needs in ageing and care” cannot supply comparable significance weights or demonstrate exhaustive coverage.

[JBI scoping-review guidance](https://jbi-global.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/MANUAL/pages/355862687/10.2.3%2BDefining%2Band%2Baligning%2Bthe%2Bobjective%2Bs%2Band%2Bquestion%2Bs) connects the review question to search, eligibility, extraction, presentation and findings through population, concept and context. [Campbell evidence-map guidance](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/cl2.1125) emphasises stakeholder consultation when establishing scope/framework and interpreting findings. [Campbell's discussion of scope versus depth](https://www.campbellcollaboration.org/2019/03/innovations-in-evidence-mapping/) explains why a broad evidence inventory is not a deep synthesis of what evidence says. [Teresa Torres](https://www.producttalk.org/opportunity-solution-trees/) uses a defined outcome and customer stories for a team's discovery tree, and warns against a company-wide tree. These are different research products; none establishes that Who Cares is a validated opportunity tree.

## Next research and presentation work

1. Preserve the broad list and stable graph, but explicitly treat much of it as a candidate inventory. Do not solve false completeness through more generated siblings.
2. Establish revisable coverage boundaries for each ICP using their responsibilities and desired outcomes, checked against published research and practitioner/lived perspectives. Include ordinary successful arrangements, exceptions, interactions, transitions and people outside familiar services. Do not call an editorial checklist exhaustive.
3. Separate broad outcomes, enabling conditions, ongoing jobs and specific obstacles during synthesis. Existing title roles are an analysis proposal; do not bulk rewrite object kinds or identities based only on label resemblance.
4. For each bounded investigation, research reach, persistence/frequency, consequence/effort, current adaptations and organised responses. Keep denominator, geography, period, source method and unknowns. Weighting depends on the decision: population reach and severity are distinct, and neither alone determines venture relevance.
5. Add explicit connections only after source review: shared context, overlap, enabling condition or documented consequence. Hypothesised causality stays hypothesised. An empty offering column is a research gap, not a commercial verdict.
6. Expose this synthesis as a concise area explanation and scoped significance/relationship context beside the current list. A deeper brief should answer what happens, why it matters, what is offered and what remains uncertain. Use scenes only when real accounts support them; otherwise describe the intended context without fabricated narrative.

The goal remains breadth plus useful, source-grounded comprehension. Passing 620 objects is not completion while evidence, context, connections, media and internal branch verification remain incomplete.
