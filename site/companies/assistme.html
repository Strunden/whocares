AssistMe

full deep dive T02

Germany · Berlin · Website: https://assistme.io
Index / file source: AgeTechX Ones to Watch; COMPANY-CLUSTERS.md · Access date for web fetches: 2026-10-05

One-line summary

Sensor clip and SaaS (alea) that tell care staff when an incontinence product needs changing, plus movement and fall signals.

Product and how it works

alea: sensor strip in incontinence products plus reusable clip; app/web notifies caregivers. Also markets movement, fall detection, and documentation. Health Capital coverage of funding calls alea a certified medical device.

How it works

Clip attaches to smart diaper/sensor strip; data to cloud; staff get smartphone/tablet/browser alerts; change when needed rather than fixed rounds only.

Customer / user, buyer, payer (scene)

User: Residents in care homes (company also mentions outpatient/clinical).

Buyer: Care home / Betreiber (B2B).

Payer: Operator CapEx/OpEx. Shared night workflow is not a default individual GKV Hilfsmittel (T02 continuum). DiPA listing: not found.

Scene: incontinence sensor for care homes

Business model and pricing

Hardware + SaaS. Public list prices: not found.

Traction signals

Deep Tech Award 2023; Innovation Award Berlin Brandenburg nomination 2025 (Berlin Partner). Brandenburg production plans (Health Capital). Facility install counts: not found on homepage.

Competitors and positioning

Our product thesis (interpreted)

Interpreted: care-home incontinence need-based change; seller AssistMe to Betreiber; payer facility. Why now: night continence is the frequent night task (Witten survey on T01/T02). Kill risk: T02 menu kill vs named shelf; Identifi already sells skip-or-change; Betreiber CapEx needs named distribution.
