Veli

full deep dive T04T08

Germany · Kassel / Munich · Website: https://veli-care.de
Index / file source: AgeTechX; COMPANY-CLUSTERS.md · Access date for web fetches: 2026-10-05

One-line summary

AI that reads existing electricity and water meters to spot inactivity or anomalies and alert contacts, without new room sensors.

Product and how it works

Uses household Strom- and Wasserzaehler patterns; alerts Pflege, Angehoerige, or Hausmeister. Positioned against classic Hausnotruf that needs active use or extra sensors.

How it works

Analyse consumption; detect danger (inactivity, leak); notify stored contacts; marketed for Service-Wohnen and private homes.

Customer / user, buyer, payer (scene)

User: Tenants in Service-Wohnen / Betreutes Wohnen and private senior households.

Buyer: Service-Wohnen operators and private households. Site quotes include DRK-related, CuraLiving, medi terra, AWO Fuldabrueck.

Payer: Operator or private. Meter-data as Hilfsmittel: not found.

Scene: meter-based passive safety for Service-Wohnen and homes

Business model and pricing

B2B Service-Wohnen + private. Prices: not found.
