Germany · Berlin · Website: https://amara.app
Index / file source: AgeTechX Ones to Watch; COMPANY-CLUSTERS.md · Access date for web fetches: 2026-10-05

One-line summary

Voice-controlled tablet and family app for older adults so they can call, hear music, and get reminders without menus.

Product and how it works

Amara Home is a voice-operated tablet that arrives pre-configured. Older adults speak to call family, play radio, hear reminders, and ask questions. Relatives manage setup via a family app. Company page: DSGVO on European servers; membership includes tablet, dock, family app, and content.

How it works

1) Device ships set up. 2) User speaks. 3) Family uses the app for photos, messages, calendar. WLAN or integrated SIM; no smartphone required for the older adult.

Customer / user, buyer, payer (scene)

User: Older adults living at home (company framing: Oma / Opa).

Buyer: Adult children / family (membership).

Payer: Private pay / family membership. No SGB XI or DiPA listing found on company page.

Scene: companionship / family connection

Business model and pricing

Membership bundling hardware and content. Exact euro list price on the public homepage fetch: not found.
