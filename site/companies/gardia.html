One-line summary

Emergency wristband with automatic fall detection for home and outdoors, without needing a smartphone.

Product and how it works

Gardia Notrufarmband: fall detection, emergency call; My Gardia App; site claims 21-day battery, 30-day trial, and 0 EUR/month with Pflegegrad (verify against Hausnotruf contract).

How it works

Wearable detects fall or user triggers; emergency process; works without smartphone (Tech.eu / DvH).
