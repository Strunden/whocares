Who Cares

A live map of ageing tech: companies, ideas, and what we killed. The map, table, and entry pages render from one JSON file, so new scans append data without HTML edits.

Entry types: company (sourced) and idea (thesis cards / themes, dashed on the map). Every factual claim cites a source; write not found instead of guessing. Interpreted theses are labelled as such.
