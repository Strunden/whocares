1. AgeTech is a frame / tag

On real maps we opened, AgeTech is almost always the outer frame, not a child of healthcare. Health, housing, finance, and caregiving sit inside it. Major databases (Crunchbase, PitchBook, Dealroom) often do not name AgeTech as a taxonomy node at all. Tracxn names an "Age Tech" sector without showing parent/siblings on the page opened.

Source: AGETECH-IN-CONTEXT.md; agetech-real-maps.md; agetech-more-maps.md (Gerontechnologist 2025, AARP, AgeTech Journal, AgeTechX market map, Silver Eco, Aging Analytics UK, etc.).

2. Altitude = one level above product categories

Altitude means stepping up from AgeTech product categories to life stage: selling the house, leaving the hospital, burial, work at retirement age. Parent themes P01-P07. They are not T19+. This step does not appear on any published map we opened.

Source: 00-MENU-AND-PLAN.md (Parent themes); AGETECH-IN-CONTEXT.md.

3. Scene = job, seller, payer

Coined here. Not a Dealroom/PitchBook/Crunchbase field. Cards sit on a scene: who does the job, who sells, who pays.

Source: AGETECH-IN-CONTEXT.md.

4. Generate many distinct what-ifs (Afeyan-style)

Around a theme, write several value hypotheses with different starting points. One starting point only searches the neighbourhood of that point. Admission means the what-if is distinct enough to take to an expert - not that the claim is true, and not a build order.

Source: afeyan-thesis-ballade-method.md; THESIS-CRITIQUE-GATE.md; THESIS-CARDS.md.

5. Kill only with an outside record

Kill on statute, price, or a named published falsifier - not on an occupied shelf alone (shelf is expert ammunition). Occupied shelf is pressure, not a pre-kill. The withdrawn six-check gate pre-killed cards for being unproven; Fabian rejected that.

Source: THESIS-CRITIQUE-GATE.md; AGETECH-IN-CONTEXT.md; AGETech-CONTINUUM-LOG.md continuum-complete (4 killed by statute).

6. Founder-written sources for the company scan

A company enters the pool only when a page this pass opened (YC company page, accelerator batch, documented hackathon/startup-weekend writeup) states the product in the founders' or the program's words. Index before name. No invented company, batch, price, or quote.

Source: RESEARCH-STATEMENTS.md S5, S7; RESEARCH-PLAN.md; COMPANY-CLUSTERS.md.

7. Stress tests: expert role + killer experiment

After admission: desk ballade speaking for an expert role (nobody emailed unless Fabian says send). Harvest why it cannot work. Only then run the named killer experiment. Status values: admitted for stress (unstressed) → stressed, still standing → killed.

Source: THESIS-CRITIQUE-GATE.md; afeyan-thesis-ballade-method.md.
